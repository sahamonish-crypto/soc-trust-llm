## Constraints carried forward if Gate 5 is frozen

1. CTU-SME-11 is the primary corpus source.
2. Study scope is network-originated SOC alerts generated by Suricata and represented in Wazuh-style form.
3. Suricata detections are never ground truth.
4. Ground truth derives from CTU provenance plus deterministic telemetry mapping plus later operational expert adjudication.
5. Unknown/background CTU cases are excluded.
6. Ground-truth stores remain separated from participant/model-visible stores.
7. C3/C4 evidence is produced from approved non-ground-truth telemetry.
8. C3 and C4 receive exactly the same evidence.
9. C4 differs from C3 only through calibrated uncertainty.
10. No live LLM is used in the confirmatory human experiment.
11. Final trial count, participant N, item composition, final episode definition, expert-adjudication results, LLM configuration, and calibration procedure remain open for later gates.
